From dimensions to G-code.

Preview hole coordinates and pocket paths, adjust the dimensions, and insert the result into the editor.

Hole patterns

Circular bolt patterns or rectangular grids. Set the center, spacing and count before inserting coordinates. Patterns are limited to 20,000 points; for grids, the X and Y counts are multiplied. An excessive pattern disables Insert and explains the limit in its tooltip.

Pocket toolpaths

Circular and rectangular pockets with depth steps, stepover and entry options. Review the preview as you change parameters.
