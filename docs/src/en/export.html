Postprocessors for your controller

Eight built-in export posts are available:

  • Milling: FANUC, SINUMERIK 840D ISO-M (G291), SINUMERIK 840D native, and Heidenhain iTNC 530.
  • Multi-axis milling: FANUC and SINUMERIK 840D native.
  • Turning: FANUC lathe A and FANUC lathe B.

The Heidenhain post outputs BEGIN/END PGM, TOOL CALL, L/FMAX, CC with C/CP, CR and drilling cycles in EXPANDED.

Customize posts in Settings → Options → Postprocessors. Select a profile on the left and edit it on the right. The buttons below the list add a copy, save, rename and delete user profiles; built-in posts remain read-only.

Save a new copy with Save or OK. The profile is checked before saving; Cancel discards unsaved changes. User profiles appear alongside the built-in posts in Export.

Five-axis FANUC ↔ SINUMERIK export is experimental. Its compensation, kinematics and verification limitations are listed in the conversion details.
