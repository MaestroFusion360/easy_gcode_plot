01 / VISUAL REVIEW

Follow every move in 3D

Step through the toolpath and jump to its source line. Bring in STL models, position and rotate them, and inspect sections in the same scene.

Start with one empty milling document. Add programs with + or Open, then switch with the document-list arrow. Each document keeps its edits, undo history, machine mode, tools, stock setup, STL scene and playback position. Close with × or Ctrl+W, with a save prompt for unsaved changes.

Options, saved libraries and WCS/Home are shared. Changed CNC settings mark old cached results as stale when selected. Finish or cancel an editing dialog before switching; Tokens follows the active document. The next launch starts a fresh session.

Compare open buffers or disk files side by side, with aligned colored differences and synchronized scrolling. Refresh rereads disk sources; the comparison remains read-only and preserves unsaved editor text.

03 / VISUAL REVIEW

Measure directly in the scene

Pick two points on toolpaths or STL geometry in Top, Front, Left or Lathe view. Snap to geometry or the grid, edit point coordinates, and read distances and coordinate differences in millimetres or inches.

Imported STL models are visual references. Full machine collision simulation is outside the current model.
